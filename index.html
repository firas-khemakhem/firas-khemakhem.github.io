// about

A release engineer moving into platform

I'm a Software Engineer at KPIT, working in C++ and Python on an automotive simulation framework used by Technica Engineering. I own the delivery path around it: GitLab CI and Jenkins pipelines, multi-stage Docker images for Linux, offline installers for Windows, and end-to-end client releases.

That work sits where DevOps meets embedded. CI jobs run as pods on Kubernetes; license-bound, SonarQube and performance-sensitive jobs are routed to dedicated machines with GitLab CI runner tags and Jenkins node labels; and every release has to work on two operating systems.

My next step is platform engineering. I'm building public proof — FrameLog first, then a small Kubernetes platform demo with Terraform, Helm, Argo CD and Prometheus/Grafana — and I'm open to DevOps/Platform roles in Tunisia, France, Germany, the Netherlands or remote across the EU.

I also spent a year as backup Product Owner, managing the Jira backlog, prioritizing with stakeholders and taking part in all Scrum ceremonies on a 6–9 person team. That is my secondary track: technical product ownership and release leadership, not a pivot away from engineering.
